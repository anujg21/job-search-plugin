# Trusted Sources

Prefer Tier 1, use Tier 2 to corroborate, use Tier 3 only for live postings and discovery. Verify that a URL or report still exists before citing; search for the latest edition rather than assuming a year.

## Tier 1: Primary data and official statistics
| Source | Use for |
|---|---|
| U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (bls.gov): Occupational Outlook Handbook, Employment Projections, OEWS wage data | 10-year growth projections, wages by occupation/metro |
| O*NET OnLine (onetonline.org) | Task/skill breakdowns per occupation, "Bright Outlook" and emerging-task data |
| World Economic Forum, Future of Jobs Report | Fastest growing/declining roles, skill shifts, employer surveys |
| LinkedIn Economic Graph / Jobs on the Rise / Workforce Reports | Title growth, skill trends, talent migration |
| Indeed Hiring Lab (hiringlab.org) | Posting volume trends, AI mentions in postings, wage trends |
| Lightcast (formerly Emsi Burning Glass) public research | Skill demand from posting data, emerging skills |
| Stanford HAI AI Index | AI adoption, AI job posting share, AI skill demand |
| Company 10-K / earnings calls / official engineering blogs | Direct signals of where firms invest and restructure |
| Government/regulator publications (NIST, EU AI Act texts, SEC, state AI laws) | Regulation creating new role requirements |

## Tier 2: Reputable research and industry analysis
- McKinsey Global Institute, Deloitte Insights, PwC (e.g. AI Jobs Barometer), BCG, Accenture research
- Gartner and Forrester (public summaries, press releases; note paywalls)
- Stack Overflow Developer Survey, CNCF Annual Survey, Dice Tech Jobs Report, CompTIA State of the Tech Workforce
- MIT Sloan Management Review, Harvard Business Review (research-backed pieces)
- Levels.fyi end-of-year reports and title/comp data (verified self-reports)
- Major outlets' reporting on hiring: Reuters, Bloomberg, WSJ, FT

## Tier 3: Live postings and discovery
- Company career pages and ATS boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters)
- LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, Wellfound, Dice, Built In
- Use for JD tallies only; cite the specific posting

## Avoid or flag
- SEO listicles ("Top 10 jobs of the future"), content farms, AI-generated career blogs
- Vendor marketing presented as research, unless the methodology is published
- Recruiter/agency blogs without data
- Reddit/forums except as anecdotal color, labeled as such
- Any stat with no traceable original source. Trace it back or drop it.

## Citation format
`[Title](URL), Publisher, Month Year`
